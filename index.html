Month-on-month payroll variance review

Compare the previous payroll with the current payroll and identify every change that requires investigation.

Issues to resolve during vetting

Check salary and deduction variations; identify duplicate staff or payroll entries; identify staff present last month but missing this month (possible exits, suspension, leave, or omission); identify new additions; and confirm unchanged records. Each difference should have an explanation, supporting evidence, an assigned action, and a resolution status before payroll clearance.

Authorisation decision — mandatory payroll control

Only entries supported by proper authorisation may be approved for payment. Where a salary, allowance, deduction adjustment, new appointment or other payroll entry lacks valid approval, mark it Rejected — Excluded from payroll so it is removed from the payable payroll total. Properly authorised and verified entries may be marked Approved for payroll. Do not permanently delete rejected entries: retain them in the audit trail with the reason, reviewer, date and evidence reference. A rejected entry must not be counted in approved totals; any removal or final approval must be completed by an authorised officer.

Three-stage salary audit approval workflow

  1. Auditor / Payroller: uploads the monthly payroll and submits each line item with its source payroll reference.
  2. Vetter: independently checks staff details, authorisation, month-on-month variations and supporting evidence; may return or disapprove an item.
  3. Super Admin: performs final approval after the auditor and vetter stages are complete.

Disapproval/removal rule: an item disapproved at any stage is marked rejected and excluded from the payable payroll totals. It is not permanently deleted; its line-item reference, reason, decision stage and audit history remain available for investigation and reporting. Only the Super Admin can give final payroll approval in this prototype.

Variation references: each identified difference is a separate traceable line item for evidence, follow-up, export and certificate reporting.
